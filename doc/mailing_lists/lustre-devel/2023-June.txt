LAD'23 - Lustre Administrators and Developers Workshop

October 04-06, 2023
Face-to-Face event, Bordeaux, France

The Lustre Administrators & Developers workshop is a great opportunity for worldwide Lustre administrators and developers to gather and exchange their experiences, developments, tools, best practices and more.

After 2 years of virtual LADs and an hybrid LAD'22 in Paris last year, we are happy to announce that we will be able to welcome you again this year for this LAD'23 edition!
This will be a face-to-face event in Bordeaux on the 5th and 6th of October 2023, preceded by the Lustre's developer summit on the 4th of October, an invitation-only event for developers to exchange about the future of Lustre.

Save the date, and keep updated of the upcoming call for presentations and the opening of registrations!

Best regards,
The LAD organization team

Web site:

We are pleased to announce that the Lustre 2.15.3 Release has been declared GA and is available for download<https://downloads.whamcloud.com/public/lustre/lustre-2.15.3/> <https://downloads.hpdd.intel.com/public/lustre/lustre-2.11.0/> . You can also grab the source from git<https://git.whamcloud.com/?p=fs/lustre-release.git;a=commit;h=3bb9e28884cce1d319eeb75b095f7938cf48c5e6>

Details of changes since 2.15.2<https://wiki.lustre.org/Lustre_2.15.2_Changelog> can be found in the 2.15.3 change log.<https://wiki.lustre.org/Lustre_2.15.3_Changelog>

There are the following notable enhancements over 2.15.2:

RHEL 8.8 is now supported for servers and clients  (LU-16755<https://jira.whamcloud.com/browse/LU-16755>).

RHEL 9.2 is now supported for clients   (LU-16756<https://jira.whamcloud.com/browse/LU-16756>)

Please log any issues found in the issue tracking system<https://jira.whamcloud.com/>.

Thanks to all those who have contributed to the creation of this release.

The next LTS release will be 2.15.4
